## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

The RESQ-AI Route Risk Engine integrates physical graph routing (via Open Source Routing Machine - OSRM) with RESQ-AI multi-layered hazard intelligence to evaluate candidate emergency response and rescue corridors. 

Rather than relying purely on shortest travel time or distance, the engine evaluates route options across three operational modes:
1. **FASTEST**: Optimizes primarily for minimum routing duration (80% time weight, 20% hazard risk weight).
2. **SAFEST**: Optimizes primarily for minimum hazard exposure (20% time weight, 80% hazard risk weight).
3. **BALANCED**: Configured equal trade-off between travel time and hazard risk (50% time weight, 50% hazard risk weight).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Mandatory Operational Disclaimers**:
> - *"Route calculation is performed by the routing engine. RESQ-AI provides hazard/risk intelligence used for route evaluation."*
> - *"Route risk is a decision-support score and is not a guarantee of road safety."*

## 3. Route Risk Scoring Methodology

For every candidate route generated by the routing engine, a composite bounded **Route Risk Score** $\in [0, 100]$ is computed using only supported RESQ-AI evidence:

$$\text{Route Risk Score} = w_1 \cdot S_{\text{hazard}} + w_2 \cdot S_{\text{susceptibility}} + w_3 \cdot S_{\text{recurrence}} + w_4 \cdot S_{\text{hydrology}} + w_5 \cdot S_{\text{multi-hazard}}$$

### Configuration Weights (`config/route_risk.yaml`)

| Component | Weight ($w_i$) | Source Dataset / Engine | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Current Hazard Risk ($S_{\text{hazard}}$)** | **0.40** | `inference/risk_engine.py` | Situational hazard risk score from Layer 1–3 IMD weather model & priors |
| **Terrain Susceptibility ($S_{\text{susceptibility}}$)** | **0.25** | `features/susceptibility/` | GFSM 30m flood susceptibility rating (Internal ordinal scaling 1–5 $\rightarrow$ 20–100) |
| **Historical Flood Recurrence ($S_{\text{recurrence}}$)** | **0.15** | `features/flood_history/` | Decoupled IFI historical flood recurrence rate per decade |
| **Hydrological Vulnerability ($S_{\text{hydrology}}$)** | **0.10** | `features/hydrology/` | CAMELS-IND runoff ratio & topsoil moisture saturation |
| **Multi-Hazard History ($S_{\text{multi-hazard}}$)** | **0.10** | `features/disaster_history/` | NASA GDIS historical disaster frequency per decade |

## 4. Operational Route Modes & Scoring Formulas

For a set of $N$ candidate routes, the operational mode score $M_i$ for route $i$ is calculated as:

$$M_i = W_{\text{time}} \cdot \left( \frac{\text{duration}_i}{\max(\text{durations})} \times 100 \right) + W_{\text{risk}} \cdot \text{RouteRiskScore}_i$$

The candidate route with the **lowest composite mode score $M_i$** is selected.

| Route Mode | $W_{\text{time}}$ | $W_{\text{risk}}$ | Selection Rationale / Objective Explanation |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **FASTEST** | **0.80** | **0.20** | *"Selected because it has the lowest estimated routing duration among available candidates."* |
| **SAFEST** | **0.20** | **0.80** | *"Selected because it has the lowest RESQ-AI hazard exposure score among available candidates."* |
| **BALANCED** | **0.50** | **0.50** | *"Selected because it provides the best configured trade-off between routing duration and hazard exposure."* |

## 6. Limitations & Non-Claims

1. **No Unverified Data**: The engine does **NOT** invent traffic congestion, road closure status, bridge structural integrity, or water depth.
2. **Graph Calculation**: Road network geometry and travel durations are calculated exclusively by OSRM; RESQ-AI scores hazard risk.
3. **No Target Leakage**: Target-derived variables (`ifi_dfsi_score`, casualties, post-event impacts) were strictly excluded from predictor features.
